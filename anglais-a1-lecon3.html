LEÇON 3 - TERMINALE A1

PASSIVE VOICE

English Grammar

I. ACTIVE VOICE ET PASSIVE VOICE

À la voix active, le sujet réalise l'action.

À la voix passive, le sujet subit l'action.
Active :
The teacher teaches English.

Passive :
English is taught by the teacher.

II. FORMATION DE LA VOIX PASSIVE

Sujet + BE conjugué + Past Participle
Le temps de la phrase active est conservé grâce à la forme du verbe BE.

Le verbe principal devient un Past Participle.

III. PRESENT SIMPLE

am / is / are + Past Participle
Active :
People speak English.

Passive :
English is spoken by people.

IV. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

am / is / are + being + Past Participle
Active :
They are building a school.

Passive :
A school is being built.

V. PRESENT PERFECT

have / has + been + Past Participle
Active :
They have finished the work.

Passive :
The work has been finished.

VI. PAST SIMPLE

was / were + Past Participle
Active :
The teacher corrected the exercises.

Passive :
The exercises were corrected by the teacher.

VII. PAST CONTINUOUS

was / were + being + Past Participle
Active :
They were repairing the road.

Passive :
The road was being repaired.

VIII. PAST PERFECT

had + been + Past Participle
Active :
They had completed the project.

Passive :
The project had been completed.

IX. FUTURE SIMPLE

will + be + Past Participle
Active :
They will build a new hospital.

Passive :
A new hospital will be built.

X. AVEC « BY »

On utilise généralement by pour indiquer l'auteur de l'action.
The book was written by the author.

The school was built by the government.
Lorsque l'auteur de l'action n'est pas important ou n'est pas connu, on peut ne pas le mentionner.

XI. MÉTHODE DE TRANSFORMATION

Étape 1 : Identifier le complément d'objet de la phrase active.

Étape 2 : Le placer comme sujet de la phrase passive.

Étape 3 : Conserver le même temps avec le verbe BE.

Étape 4 : Transformer le verbe principal en Past Participle.

Étape 5 : Ajouter by + agent si nécessaire.

🧠 À RETENIR POUR LE BAC

Present Simple : is / are + PP

Present Continuous : is / are + being + PP

Present Perfect : has / have + been + PP

Past Simple : was / were + PP

Past Continuous : was / were + being + PP

Past Perfect : had + been + PP

Future Simple : will + be + PP
