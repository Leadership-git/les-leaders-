LEÇON 1 - TERMINALE A1

TENSES

English Grammar

I. PRESENT SIMPLE

Le Present Simple est utilisé pour parler d'habitudes, de vérités générales et de faits réguliers.
Subject + Verb

He / She / It + Verb + S
I go to school every day.

She works in Abidjan.

The sun rises in the east.

II. PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Le Present Continuous décrit une action qui est en train de se dérouler.
Subject + am / is / are + Verb-ing
I am studying English.

She is reading a book.

They are playing football.

III. PRESENT PERFECT

Le Present Perfect permet notamment de parler d'une action passée ayant un lien avec le présent.
Subject + have / has + Past Participle
I have finished my homework.

She has visited Ghana.

IV. PAST SIMPLE

Le Past Simple est utilisé pour parler d'une action terminée dans le passé.
Subject + Verb-ed

ou

Subject + forme passée du verbe irrégulier
I visited my friend yesterday.

She went to school last Monday.

V. PAST CONTINUOUS

Le Past Continuous décrit une action qui était en cours à un moment du passé.
Subject + was / were + Verb-ing
I was studying at 8 p.m.

They were playing football.

VI. PAST PERFECT

Le Past Perfect permet de parler d'une action qui s'est produite avant une autre action passée.
Subject + had + Past Participle
When I arrived, she had already left.

They had finished the work before the teacher arrived.

VII. FUTURE SIMPLE

Le Future Simple permet de parler d'une action qui aura lieu dans le futur.
Subject + will + Verb
I will study tomorrow.

They will come next week.

VIII. SINCE / FOR

Since indique le point de départ d'une période.

For indique la durée d'une période.
I have lived here since 2020.

I have lived here for six years.
Since : 2020, Monday, January, last year...

For : two years, three days, five months...
