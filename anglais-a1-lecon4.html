LEÇON 4 - TERMINALE A1

REPORTED SPEECH

Direct Speech → Indirect Speech

I. DÉFINITION

Le Reported Speech permet de rapporter les paroles d'une personne sans reprendre exactement ses paroles.

On distingue :

Direct Speech : les paroles exactes sont rapportées.

Reported Speech : les paroles sont rapportées indirectement.
Direct :
John said, “I am tired.”

Reported :
John said that he was tired.

II. LES CHANGEMENTS DE TEMPS

Lorsque le verbe introducteur est au passé, le temps du verbe peut changer.

Present Simple → Past Simple

“I work hard.”
→ He said that he worked hard.

Present Continuous → Past Continuous

“I am studying.”
→ He said that he was studying.

Present Perfect → Past Perfect

“I have finished.”
→ He said that he had finished.

III. AUTRES CHANGEMENTS DE TEMPS

Past Simple → Past Perfect

“I visited Paris.”
→ He said that he had visited Paris.

Will → Would

“I will come.”
→ He said that he would come.

Can → Could

“I can swim.”
→ He said that he could swim.

IV. CHANGEMENT DES PRONOMS

Les pronoms doivent être adaptés selon la personne qui parle et la personne à qui l'on rapporte les paroles.
Mary said, “I am happy.”

→ Mary said that she was happy.

John said, “I like my school.”

→ John said that he liked his school.

V. CHANGEMENT DES EXPRESSIONS DE TEMPS

now → then

today → that day

yesterday → the day before

tomorrow → the next day

last week → the previous week

next week → the following week

here → there

VI. SAY ET TELL

Say est généralement utilisé sans complément de personne directement après le verbe.

Tell est généralement suivi d'une personne.
He said that he was tired.

He told me that he was tired.

VII. QUESTIONS AU REPORTED SPEECH

Pour rapporter une question, on ne conserve pas l'ordre interrogatif de la phrase directe.
Direct :
He asked, “Where do you live?”

Reported :
He asked me where I lived.
Pour les questions auxquelles on répond par Yes / No, on utilise généralement if ou whether.
“Do you like English?”

→ He asked me if I liked English.

VIII. ORDRES ET CONSEILS

Pour rapporter un ordre ou une demande, on utilise souvent :

tell / ask + personne + to + verbe
“Open the door.”

→ He told me to open the door.

“Please help me.”

→ She asked me to help her.

IX. MÉTHODE DE TRANSFORMATION

1. Identifier le verbe introducteur.

2. Identifier le temps de la phrase directe.

3. Adapter le temps si nécessaire.

4. Changer les pronoms.

5. Changer les expressions de temps et de lieu si nécessaire.

6. Vérifier l'ordre des mots.

🧠 À RETENIR POUR LE BAC

Present Perfect → Past Perfect

Past Simple → Past Perfect

Will → Would

Can → Could
N'oublie pas de vérifier les pronoms, les temps et les expressions de temps.
