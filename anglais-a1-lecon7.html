LEÇON 7 - TERMINALE A1

RELATIVE CLAUSES

Who — Which — Whose — That

I. DÉFINITION

Une relative clause est une proposition qui donne une information supplémentaire sur une personne, un animal ou une chose.

Elle est généralement introduite par un relative pronoun.
The student who studies hard will succeed.

→ « who studies hard » donne une information sur « the student ».

II. WHO

Who s'utilise principalement pour parler d'une personne.
The woman who teaches us is very kind.

The student who won the competition is my friend.
Person + WHO + Verb

III. WHICH

Which s'utilise principalement pour parler d'une chose ou d'un animal.
This is the book which I bought yesterday.

The car which is parked outside is mine.
Thing / Animal + WHICH + Clause

IV. THAT

That peut être utilisé pour parler d'une personne, d'un animal ou d'une chose dans certaines propositions relatives définissant le nom.
The man that lives here is a teacher.

The book that I bought is interesting.
Dans les exercices, retiens notamment :

Person → who / that

Thing → which / that

V. WHOSE

Whose exprime la possession. Il signifie généralement « dont » en français.
The student whose father is a doctor is very intelligent.

The woman whose car was stolen called the police.
Person + WHOSE + Noun

VI. CHOISIR LE BON PRONOM

WHO
→ personne

WHICH
→ chose / animal

THAT
→ personne ou chose dans certaines relatives définissant le nom

WHOSE
→ possession

VII. EXEMPLES DE TRANSFORMATION

I know the girl. She won the prize.

→ I know the girl who won the prize.
This is the phone. I bought the phone yesterday.

→ This is the phone which I bought yesterday.
I met a student. His brother lives in London.

→ I met a student whose brother lives in London.

VIII. ERREURS À ÉVITER

❌ The man which lives here...
✔ The man who lives here...

❌ The book who I bought...
✔ The book which I bought...

❌ The girl which father is a doctor...
✔ The girl whose father is a doctor...

IX. MÉTHODE POUR LE BAC

Étape 1 : Cherche le nom qui est décrit.

Étape 2 : Demande-toi s'il s'agit d'une personne, d'une chose ou d'une possession.

Étape 3 : Choisis le pronom relatif approprié.

Étape 4 : Vérifie que la phrase obtenue est grammaticalement correcte.

🧠 À RETENIR POUR LE BAC

WHO → PERSON

WHICH → THING / ANIMAL

THAT → PERSON / THING

WHOSE → POSSESSION
Exemple final :

The student who works hard will succeed.

The book which I bought is useful.

The girl whose mother is a teacher is my friend.
